AVTools Help

Help for the AVTools Suite add-in for Revit 2025 and 2026. These are the pages Revit opens when you press F1 on a BIM Acoustics ribbon button.

AV Tools panel

  • System Designer — distributed loudspeaker layout, coverage, room acoustics, circuiting, amps & cabling, coordination.
  • SpecTool — CSI Part 2 (Products) spec articles from the equipment in your model.

Utilities panel

  • Room Exchange — Revit rooms to an EASE 5 room model, and EASE loudspeakers back into Revit.
  • Lucius — the AI assistant inside the add-in.
  • License Manager — plan, trial, Validate Now, buying and changing plans.
