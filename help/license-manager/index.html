License Manager

What it does

License Manager shows your current plan and the trial, holds your Name, Company and Subscription email, and is where you validate a subscription and buy or change plans. It works with no project open.

How to open it

Revit ribbon: BIM Acoustics tab → Utilities panel → License Manager.

The trial

The free 14-day trial starts when you first open System Designer and register. It unlocks every Pro feature: circuiting, amps & cabling, coordination, SpecTool, Room Exchange and the Lucius tools. One trial per computer. During the trial License Manager shows Free trial.

After buying

  1. Subscribe from the buy links in License Manager or on the Products page.
  2. Enter the email you subscribed with in the Subscription email box and click Validate Now.
  3. The license is checked only when Validate Now is pressed, so press it again after any change to the subscription (an upgrade, or a renewal after a lapse). Entering a different email and clicking Validate Now checks that email on its own. If the license server turns a license down, License Manager says so and shows the server's reason.

Plans

  • Standard — System Designer layout, coverage and room acoustics. $60/month or $600/year.
  • RoomExchange — Room Exchange on its own. $80/month or $800/year.
  • AVTools Suite Pro — System Designer Pro, SpecTool and Room Exchange. $99/month or $990/year.

Manage or Upgrade Subscription opens the billing portal to move from one plan to another, switch monthly and annual, or cancel. Upgrading credits the unused time on your current plan. License activation needs an internet connection; once activated, System Designer and Room Exchange work offline. Billing questions: support@bimacoustics.net.
