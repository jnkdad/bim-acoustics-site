Lucius

What it does

Lucius is the AI assistant embedded inside Revit as a chat panel, powered by Claude (Anthropic). Ask by text or by voice (push-to-talk). Lucius reads your live project state through tools rather than guessing, explains the workflow, the calculations and the column meanings, and knows Room Exchange, so it can answer questions about the last export. Lucius needs an internet connection. The first time you open Lucius you are asked once whether to share anonymous transcripts, with No as the default; the tick box in the Lucius window and the one on the About tab are kept in step.

How to open it

Revit ribbon: BIM Acoustics tab → Utilities panel → Lucius.

What Lucius can read (Standard and Pro)

  • Project info (the model is named by a short alias)
  • Rooms in AV scope, or all rooms, with per-room metadata
  • Placed speakers per room
  • Rooms needing attention (unmapped materials, coverage gaps)
  • Surface-to-material mappings, and the acoustic material database

What Lucius can write (Pro only)

  • Assign an acoustic material to a surface

What Lucius does not do

  • Place speakers — that is done on the Configuration tab
  • Commit circuits, run RT60 or coverage calculations, or modify the model beyond the one material write
  • Read circuit, cabling, coordination or RT60 results — you run the calculations and read the results in the panel

What is sent

Before anything goes to the assistant, the model's name and the names of linked models are replaced with short aliases computed on your computer, and folder paths and file names are removed. Room numbers and names are sent as they are. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
