SpecTool

What it does

SpecTool generates CSI MasterFormat Part 2 (Products) spec articles from the AV equipment placed in the current Revit project, matched to your firm's own boilerplate style, filled in from datasheet PDFs, and spliced into a per-section .docx. You supply the architect's project spec template (the format: styles, fonts, headers, footers, numbering) and your own boilerplate with Part 1 and Part 3; SpecTool fills in Part 2. Divisions 11 and 27. Included with AVTools Suite Pro and in the trial; needs an internet connection.

How to open it

Revit ribbon: BIM Acoustics tab → AV Tools panel → SpecTool.

Before you start

  • AV families placed in the model, each Type with a Model value (ideally Manufacturer too). Loaded but unplaced families are ignored.
  • The architect's spec template (.docx) and your boilerplate spec (.docx) with Part 1 and Part 3. No boilerplate for 27 41 00? Use the bundled UFGS starter.
  • A folder of datasheet PDFs with the model number in each filename, and an output folder.

Your first spec in six screens

  1. Home — SpecTool scans the model and lists the spec sections your equipment needs. Click Work on it →. A one-off item that created its own section can be folded into your main section with Merge….
  2. Setup — pick the Section, enter the Phase and Issue date, leave Detail level on User Boilerplate Detail, and choose the template, boilerplate, datasheet folder and output folder. All remembered for the project.
  3. Products — untick anything that doesn't belong, fix the Category on unclassified rows (or Classify unknowns (AI)). Missing items: check the Skipped banner, + Add from model… or + Add manually….
  4. Data & Datasheets — only products missing key specs appear; most match a PDF in your datasheet folder automatically. Otherwise From URL…, Attach… or Find datasheets on the web (AI).
  5. Categories & Articles — confirm each product lands in the right Part 2 article. Corrections are remembered.
  6. Review & Generate — Generate with AI (preview), read it, untick or edit paragraphs, then Create / Update spec and Open document.

You get a .docx in the template's format: Part 1 and Part 3 from your boilerplate, generated Part 2 highlighted yellow for review, and the header/footer fields filled in. On the next issue, run SpecTool again: it updates the last issued document and keeps your hand edits outside the Part 2 articles.
