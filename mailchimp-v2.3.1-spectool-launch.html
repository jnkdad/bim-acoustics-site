Meet SpecTool.

AVTools System Designer v2.3.1 — CSI Part 2 (Products) generation for the AV vertical.

Revit + spec integration is a real category. Deltek e-SPECS, RIB SpecLink, Deltek Specpoint, Chalkline VisiSpecs — all of them link Revit models to specifications and do serious work on the architectural side.

What none of them do is the AV vertical. They're architectural-focused, library-based, and enterprise-priced. They don't understand loudspeaker classification, don't ship with UFGS 27 41 00 + AVIXA standards bundled, and they pull spec language from their library — not your firm's voice.

SpecTool is different in two ways: it's built specifically for AV / security consultants working in Revit (Divisions 11, 27, 28), and it generates CSI MasterFormat Part 2 (Products) articles from your model in your firm's own boilerplate voice — not from a subscription library.

SpecTool respects three authorities you already answer to.

1. The architect's project spec template — format authority.
You upload it once per project. It supplies styles, fonts, headers, footers, and page numbering. Your generated spec ends up looking like every other section in the architect's book.

2. Your firm's own boilerplate spec — content authority.
You upload it per section. It supplies Part 1 (General) and Part 3 (Execution) exactly the way you already write them. SpecTool lifts these unchanged and matches Part 2 to your own voice.

3. Your Revit model — product data authority.
The equipment placed in the model is the source of truth for what goes in Part 2 — at what quantities, from which manufacturers, at what performance parameters.

SpecTool fills in only Part 2, matched to the depth and voice of a Part 2 article from your own boilerplate. It doesn't invent products, doesn't override the architect's format, and doesn't rewrite Part 1 or Part 3.

Four detail levels for Part 2:

  • User Boilerplate Detail (default) — matches the depth, voice, and field selection of your own Part 2 articles.
  • Basis of Design — "Basis of Design: <mfg> <model>" opener, registered key spec bullets, "Substitutions per Section 01 25 00" closer.
  • Minimal Spec — leanest. Type + one PR2 sentence + Acceptable Products + substitutions.
  • All Mfg Specs — every performance value as a PR3 bullet.

Bundled UFGS 27 41 00 (February 2026) starter if you don't have your own boilerplate for a section — drops in the verbatim NAVFAC UFGS Part 1 and Part 3, plus a curated AVIXA supplement (V201.01, V202.01, F202.01, RP-C303.01).

Data confidentiality by test harness. Project name, building name, owner, architect, consultant, and any address or geographic identifier are never sent to Anthropic — enforced before every outgoing prompt.

Create / Update mode. First issue creates a spec against the project template. Subsequent issues update the last-issued .docx — tool-owned articles get re-generated; anything you hand-edited outside them is preserved.
