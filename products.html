Products

BIM Acoustics tools are built to accelerate real-world Revit-based AV workflows with clear design intent and transparent assumptions.
Room Exchange — Revit rooms to EASE 5. v3.1.0 adds Revit 2027.
Tick the rooms, click Export, and open a closed EASE 5 room model with one layer per surface material. Room Exchange fills each room's air and finds every surface the air touches — floors, walls, ceilings, roofs, glass, doors, columns, stairs, curtain walls and operable partitions, in the model and in its links — and seals the small gaps. Curved ceilings and roofs go out as flat panels of a size you set. Custom Perimeter handles the spaces no room describes: open lobbies, atriums, stadium bowls. Import Loudspeakers brings positions and aims from EASE back into Revit. The same engine now reads the rooms for System Designer's RT60, so the acoustics get every surface and the true air volume. Available on its own as the RoomExchange plan or in AVTools Suite Pro. One installer for Revit 2025, 2026 and 2027; press F1 on any ribbon button for help. Requires AutoCAD on the same machine — Room Exchange uses it only to convert the exported DXF to the DWG that EASE 5 imports. Supported with EASE 5; the DXF and DWG files also appear to import into Bose Modeler and ODEON, and may work in other applications that read 3D polylines or 3D faces, but those are not fully tested or supported.

Release Notes

v3.1.0 Revit 2025, 2026 & 2027 · November 2026 Current
  • Revit 2027. System Designer, SpecTool and Room Exchange run in Revit 2027 as well as 2025 and 2026, from one installer.
  • F1 help. Hover over any button on the BIM Acoustics tab and press F1 for its help page. Two panels: AV Tools (System Designer, SpecTool) and Utilities (Room Exchange, Lucius, License Manager).
  • Room Exchange loudspeaker targets. Tick Include loudspeakers and each System Designer loudspeaker goes to EASE 5 as a small target face on the AVTOOLS-SPEAKER-TARGETS layer, ready to place an EASE loudspeaker on. On Revit 2025 and 2026 the separate build process now needs the .NET 8 Desktop Runtime 8.0.32 or later; Room Exchange checks and links to the download.
  • Sharper iso-maps and fixes. Each loudspeaker gets its own coverage contour (checked against EASE 5); iso-maps draw in a second view; messages open in front of the window; Lucius transcript sharing is off unless you turn it on.
  • Security. System.IO.Packaging 8.0.1, an unused .NET 6 component removed, and every DLL and EXE in the installer signed by J. Stevens BIM Acoustics LLC. File hashes are on the download page.
