Echea Beta — ray-traced room acoustics, straight from the Revit model.
Echea is the room-acoustics add-in of BIM Acoustics Studio. It traces sound rays through a room straight from the Revit model. Tick one or more rooms; Echea takes every surface the air of those rooms touches, gives each an absorption from the materials database it shares with System Designer (over 2,500 materials, plus your own), and traces rays from an omnidirectional source or from the model's own loudspeakers to the listening positions. Reading and tracing leave the Revit model as it is.
  • Per seat and per frequency band: reverberation (EDT, T20, T30), clarity (C50, C80, D50), speech intelligibility (STI, ALcons), level (direct level, direct-to-reverberant ratio, sound strength G) and spaciousness (LF, LFC, IACC).
  • Rooms straight from Revit, including rooms in linked models and Room Exchange Custom Perimeters. Several rooms ticked together are read as one space, with a 3D preview coloured by material.
  • Sources and receivers: an omnidirectional source where the talker stands, or the model's loudspeakers with CLF or EASE loudspeaker data; picked seats and points, listener seats, a binaural listener, and audience areas drawn over the seating. Gain, EQ and delay per loudspeaker group.
  • See the sound: the reflections arriving at a seat as a “hedgehog”, single reflection paths, and an animation of the sound spreading through the room that can be saved as a video. Maps over the audience or the room's surfaces, drawn in a Revit 3D view or written to the model.
  • Runs: save a run and reopen it without tracing again, compare runs, export tables as CSV, and export the room as DXF or DWG. A Sabine, Eyring and Arau-Puchades check runs before tracing.
  • Listen to a clip as it would sound at a seat, for one or two ears. This is the least finished part of the Beta.
This is a Beta. Echea has been checked against a reference in two rooms only: a church compared with EASE 5 AURA, where EDT, T30, C80, D50, G and STI agree closely, and a measured auditorium from a public benchmark, where T30 came out about 12 percent longer than measured and C80 within about 1 dB. Other kinds of room have not been compared. IACC uses a simple model of the head, so its figures are for comparing seats and runs. Listen has not been checked by ear. Use Echea's results for trying rooms and reporting what you find, not yet for basing a design decision on, and send your findings to support@bimacoustics.net.
Echea installs with AVTools System Designer v3.2.0 from the same installer (Revit 2025, 2026 and 2027). A 14-day free trial of Echea starts the first time it is opened on a PC, separate from System Designer's own trial. After the trial, Echea is available on the Echea and BIM Acoustics Studio plans. The name: echea (Greek, “sounders”) were the bronze vessels the Roman architect Vitruvius describes in ancient theatres, set among the seats and tuned to ring with the performers' voices.

Room Exchange — Revit rooms to EASE 5. v3.2.0 writes simpler, lighter drawings.
Tick the rooms, click Export, and open a closed EASE 5 room model with one layer per surface material. Room Exchange fills each room's air and finds every surface the air touches — floors, walls, ceilings, roofs, glass, doors, columns, stairs, curtain walls and operable partitions, in the model and in its links — and seals the small gaps. Curved walls, ceilings and roofs go out as flat panels of a size you set, and detail too small to matter acoustically is left out, so the drawing has fewer faces and fewer slivers. Custom Perimeter handles the spaces no room describes: open lobbies, atriums, stadium bowls. Import Loudspeakers brings positions and aims from EASE back into Revit. The same engine now reads the rooms for System Designer's RT60, so the acoustics get every surface and the true air volume. Available on its own as the RoomExchange plan or in AVTools Suite Pro. One installer for Revit 2025, 2026 and 2027; press F1 on any ribbon button for help. Requires AutoCAD on the same machine — Room Exchange uses it only to convert the exported DXF to the DWG that EASE 5 imports. Supported with EASE 5: open the DWG in EASE 5. DXF is also written for programs that read only DXF; other programs that read DXF or DWG have been tried but are not supported.

Release Notes

v3.2.0 Revit 2025, 2026 & 2027 · November 2026 Current

AVTools System Designer 3.2.0

  • STI mapping on the Room Acoustics tab, over the same grid as the SPL coverage map: STI at every grid point from the loudspeakers' direct field, the room's reverberant field and its background noise, per octave band from 125 Hz to 8 kHz with the IEC 60268-16 male weighting. Target STI 0.60 by default, with a table of minimum, average and maximum STI and the share of the floor at or above the target.
  • Map options: 1, 2 or 3 dB between SPL iso-lines; optional heat-map fill for the SPL and STI maps; each map drawn in its own copy of the plan for its own sheet; a 3D coverage carpet that shows in 3D views, Autodesk Construction Cloud and Navisworks; Export PNG for the maps and the RT60 graph.
  • Every loudspeaker in the room counts in the maps and STI figures: placed by System Designer, by hand or in a linked model. Circuiting, cabling and schedules still take only System Designer's loudspeakers.
  • Rooms: material assignments are kept in the Revit model and shared with Echea; one left-out list shared by System Designer, Room Exchange and Echea; Room Exchange Custom Perimeters usable for room acoustics and the STI map; room heights follow the level's height (fixes models with levels set from the Survey Point).
  • Loudspeaker layout (new, still being tested): narrow rooms with nooks laid out part by part, curved rooms on their real outline, a choice of ceiling zone with a loudspeaker family for each, and hosting on an exposed deck or a sloped face. Please send what you find to support@bimacoustics.net.
  • More: Categories... adds Specialty Equipment, Electrical Fixtures, Electrical Equipment or Data Devices to the loudspeaker list; the automatic tap picks the smallest tap that meets the required power; Commit warns when a circuit has no cable length or loss; Lucius answers before System Designer is opened.

Room Exchange

  • Simpler drawings: detail too small to matter acoustically is left out, for about 40 percent fewer faces on a room with a lot of curved geometry and 10 to 15 percent on a mostly flat room. The room stays closed.
  • Curved walls go out as flat strips along their curve; Custom Perimeters and the left-out list are shared with System Designer and Echea; room heights follow the level's height.
  • Supported with EASE 5: open the DWG in EASE 5. DXF is also written for programs that read only DXF.

Echea (Beta)

  • First Beta of the room-acoustics add-in of BIM Acoustics Studio: ray-traced results per seat and per band, hedgehogs and animation, straight from the Revit model. Results are for trying rooms and reporting what you find, not yet for design decisions. More about Echea.

Upgrading: close Revit and run the installer; no uninstall needed. Projects, licence, settings and EULA acceptance carry forward. Existing projects open with a 2 dB step and no fill, exactly as before. Rooms of the kinds under “Loudspeaker layout” can get a different loudspeaker count and positions the next time Refresh Calculations is pressed.

v3.1.0 Revit 2025, 2026 & 2027 · November 2026
  • Revit 2027. System Designer, SpecTool and Room Exchange run in Revit 2027 as well as 2025 and 2026, from one installer.
